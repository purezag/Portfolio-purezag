Design notesFour rules

OHMEN studio, 2026

Drawn fromthe lightoutward

We draw the lamps before anything else. An electric car has no grille to announce it, so the light has to. Four rules came out of that, and every OHMEN follows them.

  1. N.01Light first
  2. N.02Paint is a mirror
  3. N.03One family
  4. N.04Silence is designed

01

N.01

Light first

The ARC's front signature is a single strip of LEDs, 2 mm tall and nearly a metre and a half wide. Every surface around it was shaped to frame that line: the bonnet falls towards it and the fenders wrap it.

Line height
2 mm
Width
1,460 mm
Colour
6,500 K

02

N.02

Paint is a mirror

Puro Sangue is a three-stage paint. A silver base gives it depth, a red-tinted lacquer gives it colour and a clear coat polished by hand lets reflections run without a ripple. We light it with rectangular frames so every curve reads as one clean edge.

Stages
3
Clear coat
60 µm
Finish
By hand

N.03

One family

UMBRA follows the same rules at a larger scale. Its gloss black body has almost no colour of its own: you only see its shape where the light catches it, which is exactly the point. Same signature, a hand's width higher.

04

N.04

Silence is designed

With nothing to cool or exhaust, the loudest thing at speed is the tyres. We tuned the cabin to 58 dB and built the pass-by sound you hear on this site from the motors' own frequencies, so the car sounds like what it is.
